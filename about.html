About Me

Hi, I’m Cory Black. I’m a soccer freestyle and juggling performer based in Seattle with 16 years of freestyle experience and over 11 years of juggling. I’ve performed in over 300 live shows around the U.S., including stage productions, festivals, corporate events, and sports venues.

I’ve competed in freestyle football competitions around the world, including the Super Ball World Freestyle Football Championships in Prague. I also spent two years performing with the Flynn Creek Circus, where I’ve toured as both a solo and ensemble act.

Past clients include the Seattle Sounders, Moisture Festival, McDonald’s, and a variety of festivals and private events. I’m available for shows, promotions, and creative projects — get in touch here.
