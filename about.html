Where I Am Now

I’m based in Seattle, performing freestyle football and juggling for corporate events, festivals, theaters, sporting events, and touring circus productions around the country.

Most recently, I earned 3rd Place at the 2026 International Jugglers’ Association Championships. I’m always glad to connect about new opportunities to share the act with a new audience.

About Me

Hi, I’m Cory Black. I’m a soccer freestyle and juggling performer based in Seattle with 16 years of freestyle experience and over 11 years of juggling. I’ve performed in over 300 live shows around the U.S., including stage productions, festivals, corporate events, and sports venues.

I’ve competed in freestyle football competitions around the world, including the Super Ball World Freestyle Football Championships in Prague.

I also spent two years performing with the Flynn Creek Circus, where I’ve toured as both a solo and ensemble act.

Past clients include the Seattle Sounders, Moisture Festival, McDonald’s, and a variety of festivals and private events.

I’m available for shows, promotions, and creative projects — get in touch here.
